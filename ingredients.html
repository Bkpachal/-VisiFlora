VisiFlora Ingredients

Review the ingredient groups described for VisiFlora and verify the current Supplement Facts label before use.

Carotenoids

Marketing materials identify lutein, zeaxanthin, astaxanthin and lycopene as formula components.

Botanical Ingredients

Ingredients referenced in product information include bilberry, saffron, ginkgo biloba and grape seed extract. Confirm the current label for exact amounts and formulation.

Vitamins and Minerals

Vitamins C and E, zinc, copper, selenium and other nutrients are described in formula information. Do not assume that every ingredient is present in every current product batch.

Ask a qualified healthcare professional about interactions, allergies and suitability before use.
