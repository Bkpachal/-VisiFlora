Explore the general eye-wellness support areas associated with the VisiFlora multi-nutrient formula.

Everyday Eye Wellness

VisiFlora is marketed as a daily nutritional supplement for adults interested in general eye wellness and visual comfort. It is not a treatment for eye disease.

Antioxidant Nutritional Support

The formula is described as including antioxidant vitamins, carotenoids, minerals and botanical ingredients. Ingredient research does not establish clinical results for the finished product.

Healthy-Aging Routine

A supplement should complement, not replace, regular eye examinations, a balanced diet, appropriate lighting and healthy screen habits.
